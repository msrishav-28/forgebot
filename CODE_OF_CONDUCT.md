# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

Expected behavior includes:

- Being considerate and constructive.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and receiving feedback professionally.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and code, not people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, sexualized conduct, threats, and deliberate disruption.

Report concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact listed in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This project adapts the Contributor Covenant principles. The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
